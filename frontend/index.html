Selected Retinal Structure

Optic Nerve Head (Optic Disc)

The exit point for retinal ganglion cell axons leaving the eye to transmit visual information to the occipital cortex.

Associated Clinical Pathologies:
Glaucomatous cupping, disc pallor, neuroretinal rim thinning, and papilledema.
Screening Relevance:
Primary diagnostic site for Glaucoma screening (CDR estimation).
Click any pulsating marker (1-4) on the fundus photograph to explore anatomical details.

Supported Retinal Conditions

Category D

Diabetic Retinopathy

Microvascular retinal damage caused by prolonged hyperglycemia. Characterized by microaneurysms, hemorrhages, exudates, and neovascularization.

AI Targets: Microaneurysms, blot hemorrhages
Category G

Glaucoma

Optic neuropathy causing progressive peripheral visual field loss, frequently linked to elevated intraocular pressure and increased cup-to-disc ratio.

AI Targets: Optic disc excavation, CDR > 0.6
Category C

Cataract

Progressive clouding or opacification of the crystalline crystalline lens causing generalized blurring and reduced fundus visualization.

AI Targets: Media opacity, loss of vessel detail
Category A

AMD (Macular Degeneration)

Degenerative macular condition affecting central high-acuity vision, marked by drusen deposition, RPE atrophy, or choroidal neovascularization.

AI Targets: Macular drusen, geographic atrophy
Category H

Hypertensive Retinopathy

Retinal vascular changes secondary to chronic arterial hypertension, including arteriolar attenuation, copper-wiring, and AV crossing nicking.

AI Targets: Focal arteriolar narrowing, AV nicking
